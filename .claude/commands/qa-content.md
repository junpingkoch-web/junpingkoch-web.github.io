Review $ARGUMENTS for:
1. Does the H1/title match what the content actually delivers — no bait-and-switch?
2. Heading hierarchy: H2s cover distinct subtopics, no orphan/redundant H3s
3. Generic filler sentences that could apply to any topic — cut them
4. Overused AI-writing tells (repeated "In today's world...", excessive em dashes, listy padding, "it's important to note that") — flag and suggest a specific rewrite for each
5. If there are factual/numeric claims (prices, rates, dates), are they sourced or hedged appropriately, matching this project's existing disclosure conventions from CLAUDE.md
